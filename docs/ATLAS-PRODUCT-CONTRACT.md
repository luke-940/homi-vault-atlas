# Atlas Product Contract — v7.8

## Mission

Atlas Builder translates the Vault into a product that Homi colleagues can
discover in three seconds, understand in thirty seconds, and trust within three
minutes. The primary product is the Public Embassy Edition. The Owner
Intelligence Edition is a deeper local companion built from the same compiler.

## Public knowledge admission

The public graph admits safe real titles and actual directed references from:

- MOC
- Papers
- Signals
- Rocket
- Groot
- Intelligence Layer
- Strategy Insights that have at least one resolved reference to another
  admitted core domain

The main graph excludes Strategy Requests, Console, raw Daily and dated research
notes, archives, backups, templates, control records, receipts, scaffolding,
document bodies, frontmatter, internal paths, PII, secrets, command history, and
live operating state.

An admitted title is never masked. A rejected title is absent and appears only
as a counted exclusion reason. The compiler must prove:

`physical = named + aggregate + excluded` and `unclassified = 0`.

## Objective graph meaning

- Position: deterministic topology and domain structure.
- Size and local light: unique inbound documents.
- Color: domain.
- Shape: node kind.
- Thin line: actual directed reference.
- Line strength: occurrence count.
- Arrow: local or selected direction only.
- Agency line: stewardship or contribution, never a knowledge relation.
- Homi: product provenance, never knowledge gravity.

Dates are optional inspector metadata. They do not control node admission,
position, radius, glow, or default visibility.

## One space, four lenses

1. **Homi Vault** (`whole-vault`) — all admitted nodes and a quiet field of actual references.
2. **Knowledge Core** — MOC, Papers, Signals, and their cross-domain evidence.
3. **Project Frontiers** — Rocket, Groot, Intelligence Layer, and honest gaps.
4. **Agent Stewardship** — ownership and circulation with separate grammar.

Lenses share one graph, coordinate set, selection, and URL codec. They change
camera bookmarks, label rank, and emphasis only.

## Curated directory

Explore includes `Graph · Structure · List`. Structure is a direct, accessible
hierarchy derived from the admitted Vault folders and titles. It preserves
folder parentage but removes numeric ordering prefixes from visible folder
labels. Console, raw Daily, dated notes, receipts, QA ledgers, queues,
changelogs, and other operating branches are not presented as knowledge
structure. Their omission is counted and explained; no hidden branch is
reinterpreted as another folder. Folder hierarchy explains location, while the
graph remains the sole source for relationship direction and strength.

## Interaction

- `previewId` is local and transient.
- `focusId` is committed and URL-backed.
- Hover and keyboard preview do not change camera, layout, URL, or label packing.
- Click, Enter, and tap commit selection.
- Escape restores the previous semantic selection and camera.
- Search preview uses the current stage before navigation.
- Mobile replaces hover with tap, focus, step-through, and a bottom sheet.

## Profiles

- Public: safe actual names, actual public relationships, no owner-only bytes.
- Owner: all admitted local titles and deeper metrics, `noindex`, no telemetry.
- Public source IDs are a subset of Owner source IDs.
- Both profiles must produce deterministic semantic, layout, and projection
  digests from the same captured input.
